# Product

register: brand

## Users
Two separate worlds. Customers: people in Italy (and English speakers) booking a barber or salon from a phone, often on the street, in daylight, in a hurry. Professionals: barbers and salon owners running the shop, checking the calendar between clients.

## Product purpose
torni ("I'll come back") is a booking platform for local barbers and salons. Customers find a shop and book in seconds. Owners manage services, hours and appointments.

## Brand
Three words: warm, quick, handmade. Confident and local, like a good shop sign, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references
Fresha (white page, black pill buttons, purple gradient, centered search pill). Any purple/indigo gradient. Barbershop clichés (black and gold, vintage mustaches). Beige editorial.

## Strategic principles
- Customer world is daylight and drenched in one warm vermilion; business world is dark ink. Same accent, different mood.
- Rectangles with small radii, not pills. Left-aligned, asymmetric layouts.
- Demo mode: no real security; flows must never dead-end.
